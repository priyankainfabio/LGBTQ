Curated Itinerary

A cinematic route,
not a checklist.

Day 01–02

Delhi

Arrival, Old Delhi, Sikh temple, Chandni Chowk, India Gate, Humayun’s Tomb.

Day 03

Khajuraho

Temple carvings, same-sex expression, sacred heritage and ancient intimacy.

Day 04–05

Agra & Jaipur

Moonlight garden, Taj Mahal sunrise, Bharatpur, Rambagh Palace / Jai Mahal Palace.

Day 06

Jaipur + Hijra Community

Amber Fort, City Palace, and respectful interaction with India’s traditional third-gender community.

Day 07–08

Shahpura Bagh

Slow luxury, lakeside walks, village encounters, yoga, privacy, and inclusive calm.

Day 09–10

Udaipur

Lake Pichola, City Palace, miniature painting, Taj Lake Palace / Lalit Laxmi Vilas.

Day 11–14

Ahmedabad · Vadodara · Rajpipla

Lakshmi Vilas Palace, lunch with Prince Manvendra Singh, Gazra Café, farewell dinner.
